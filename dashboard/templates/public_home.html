FROM VOICE TO ACTION

Every request deserves a clear path.

A simple, traceable workflow can help public services respond more effectively.

01

Share a need

A citizen submits a service request or community report.

02

Route responsibly

The request is recorded and directed to the responsible service team.

03

Take action

An authorised officer reviews the case and records progress.

04

Keep informed

Citizens can receive updates and follow the outcome where available.

This describes the intended workflow. Availability depends on the relevant module and authorised institutional processes.
